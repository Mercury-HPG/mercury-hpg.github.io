Research

What the group works on.

  • Large-scale data reduction

    Error-bounded lossy compression for scientific data, and the question of what a reconstruction has to preserve to stay useful.

  • GPU-accelerated compression

    Compressor design for modern accelerators: prediction, quantization, and entropy coding that actually parallelize.

  • In situ neural-hybrid reconstruction

    Learning-based methods that improve reconstruction quality alongside a classical compressor rather than replacing it.

  • HPC/AI cyberinfrastructure

    Moving scientific data across HPC, cloud, and edge, and the software that has to hold up while it does.

Publications are listed on the PI's page; the code is under Projects.
